## Define before acquiring

A timing result needs named channels, edge polarity, threshold method, reference, acquisition mode, and sign convention.

## Separate resolution and repeatability

Displayed digits are not automatically measurement accuracy. Repeatability, timebase quality, trigger behavior, interpolation, probe loading, and signal integrity can all contribute.
